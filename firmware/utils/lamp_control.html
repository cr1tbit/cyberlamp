How to use

Use Chrome or Edge (desktop or Android). Web Bluetooth only works on pages served over https:// or from http://localhost, for example: python3 -m http.server in this directory, then open http://localhost:8000/lamp_control.html.

White dims with software PWM on the LED drivers' output enable. Spread keeps the outputs on all the time and sets the brightness by the number of lit LEDs, spread over the lamp, so Bluetooth activity can't make it flicker. Colour temperature applies to both white modes; each mode remembers its own brightness.

Changes made with the knob don't show up here by themselves: press Read state, or enable the periodic refresh. Settings are saved to the lamp's flash two seconds after the last change.

Console commands: br 0..40 brightness, mx 0..40 colour mix, md wh|sp|uv|ir mode, st status, he help.
